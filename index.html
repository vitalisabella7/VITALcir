VITAL Public Curriculum

Presented by the VITAL organization, founded by Isabella Huynh and brough to you with a student-led initiative.

UNIT 1 — Foundations & Fundamentals of Wellness (School Transitioning Period) (Aug–Sept)

  • Program introduction and expectations
  • What mental health and wellness mean
  • School stress and adjustment
  • Emotional wellness basics
  • Introduction to peer support and safe school culture
  • Basic stress response in the body (fight-or-flight response)

Activities: Icebreakers, stress mapping exercises, coping skills brainstorming, group reflection discussions

UNIT 2 — Brain, Body & Stress Response (Oct–Nov)

  • Anxiety and how it affects the body and mind
  • Burnout and stress overload
  • Sleep and mental health connection
  • Emotional regulation skills
  • Nervous system response
  • Stress hormones (cortisol/adrenaline)
  • Physical symptoms of stress (headaches, fatigue, racing heart, etc.)

Activities: Myth vs fact games, coping strategy building, scenario-based discussions, guest speaker session (counselor, nurse, therapist, EMS)

UNIT 3 — Communication & Peer Support (Dec–Jan)

  • Healthy communication skills
  • Active listening
  • Supporting friends safely and appropriately
  • Boundaries in relationships and friendships
  • Recognizing when someone may need help
  • Emotional regulation basics
  • Stress and behavior changes
  • Intro-level trauma-informed awareness

Activities: Roleplay scenarios, peer support practice activities, discussion circles, reflection exercises

UNIT 4 — Awareness, Safety & Support Systems (Feb–Mar)

  • School wellness and supportive environments
  • Awareness of student well-being and safety culture
  • Recognizing general signs someone may be struggling
  • Importance of trusted adults and support systems
  • Behavioral changes linked to stress or emotional distress
  • General understanding of early warning signs

Activities: Awareness campaign creation, group discussions, poster or message design activities, guest speaker session

UNIT 5 — Leadership & Wellness Reflection (Apr–May)

  • Student leadership in wellness and awareness
  • Creating positive school culture
  • Reflection on personal growth and learning
  • End-of-year program review

Activities: Group projects, presentations, reflection discussions, celebration and closing session
